Memory Notes · a map of memory technology

Memory: from one bit to one chip

Study notes on SRAM, DRAM, NAND flash and emerging memories. They follow a memory chip from device physics through circuit design, system applications, wafer processing, test and packaging to reliability. Every chapter has diagrams and a glossary, and links to interactive experiments where you can change the parameters yourself.
